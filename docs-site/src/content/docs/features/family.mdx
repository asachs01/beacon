# Family Members

Family members are the people in your household. Each member gets a name, an avatar, a color, a role, and an optional PIN. Members are used for chore assignment, the leaderboard, and visual identification throughout the app.

---

## Adding a family member

1. Click the **gear icon** (Settings) in the bottom of the sidebar
   - On mobile, tap **More** in the bottom tab bar, then tap **Settings**
2. The "Family Members" modal opens showing your current members (or "No family members yet" if none exist)
3. Click **+ Add Member**
4. Fill in the form:
   - **Name**: Enter the member's name (required)
   - **Avatar**: Pick an emoji from the categorized grid (see below)
   - **Color**: Pick a color from the 8-color palette
   - **Role**: Select Parent or Child
   - **PIN**: Optionally enter a 4-6 digit numeric PIN. Once saved, it shows as **PIN set** (see [PIN setup](#pin-setup))
   - **Privilege switch** (children only, **1.45.0** and later): Optionally pick a Home Assistant switch, toggle, light, or fan that turns on when today's chores are done. The default is None. See [Chore privileges](/docs/features/chore-privileges/).
5. Click **Add Member**

The member is saved immediately to the browser's localStorage and (when possible) synced to Home Assistant input_text helpers.

## Roles

Each family member has one of two roles:

| Role | Description |
|------|-------------|
| **Parent** | Full access. The presence of at least one parent enables the "Add Chore" button in the Chores panel. |
| **Child** | Standard access. Can complete and uncomplete chores. |

Roles are currently informational and used to control UI visibility (e.g., showing the chore creation form). There is no access control enforced by roles at this time. Child members can also have an optional privilege switch; that does not change who can open Settings.

## PIN setup

PINs are optional 4-6 digit numeric codes. When a PIN is set:

- Only digits are accepted (letters and symbols are stripped during entry)
- The maximum length is 6 digits
- From Beacon **X.Y.Z**, Beacon keeps only a salted hash of the PIN on the Beacon server, in `beacon_family_members.json`. The PIN itself is not saved anywhere.
- Screens and devices only learn whether a member has a PIN. They never receive the PIN or its hash.

There is nothing new to set up. PINs saved by an earlier version are converted to hashes the first time Beacon starts after the update. Other member details are left as they are. The log notes only how many PINs were converted, for example `[beacon] migrated 2 family PINs`.

### Changing or removing a PIN

From Beacon **X.Y.Z**, a saved PIN can't be shown again, not even in Settings. When you edit a member who has a PIN, the form shows **PIN set** with two buttons:

- **Change**: enter a new 4-6 digit PIN, then choose **Save Changes**. If you leave the new PIN empty, the current PIN stays as it is.
- **Remove**: clears the PIN when you save.

Saving without choosing either button leaves the PIN as it is.

### What a family PIN unlocks

Any configured family PIN unlocks these parent actions:

- Revealing navigation while Beacon's kiosk mode is on
- Leaving [Kid Display](/docs/features/kid-display/)
- Leaving [guest mode](/docs/features/guest-mode/)
- Approving or rejecting a [Magic Import](/docs/features/magic-import/)
- [Pairing a device](/docs/getting-started/pairing/), along with the 6-digit pairing code

If no PIN is set, kiosk navigation appears as soon as the hidden gesture succeeds, and Kid Display asks for a simple confirmation instead. The privilege switch does not use the PIN. Checking a chore does not ask for one.

From Beacon **X.Y.Z**, every family PIN check happens on the Beacon server, so the screen needs to reach Beacon. For kiosk navigation, Kid Display, leaving guest mode, and Magic Import, if the screen can't reach Beacon (a network error, a server error, or no answer within about 8 seconds), it stays where it is and shows "Can't reach Beacon. Check the connection and try again." Nothing is checked on the device instead. Try again once the connection is back.

Wrong PINs are limited per device, the same way as [pairing](/docs/getting-started/pairing/#if-pairing-is-locked), but counted separately. A child trying PINs on one screen doesn't lock pairing, and doesn't lock PIN checks on another screen.

The [guest PIN](/docs/features/guest-mode/) is separate. It cannot be the same digits as a family PIN, and it never opens those family-PIN gates. A family PIN leaves guest mode. When no family PIN is set, the guest PIN leaves it.

:::caution[Change PINs after updating]
Before Beacon **X.Y.Z**, a paired device could read family PINs. If you've paired a device you don't fully trust, change your family PINs after you update. Open **Settings → Family Members**, edit each person, choose **Change**, and enter a new PIN.
:::

## Calendar assignment

Each family member can be linked to a Home Assistant calendar entity. This powers the per-member columns in the **Family** dashboard layout (see [Dashboard](/docs/features/dashboard/)).

### Assigning a calendar

1. Open **Settings > Family Members**
2. Click the **pencil icon** on the member's card
3. In the edit form, find the **Calendar** dropdown — it lists all calendar entities discovered from your Home Assistant instance
4. Select the calendar that belongs to this member
5. Click **Save Changes**

Once assigned, the member's dashboard column will show only events from their linked calendar. Events from calendars that are not assigned to any member appear in the **"Other"** column.

### Tips for calendar setup

- **Google Calendar**: Each Google account connected to HA automatically creates calendar entities (e.g., `calendar.john_gmail_com`). Assign the appropriate one to each family member.
- **Kids without accounts**: If a child doesn't have their own Google account, create a shared sub-calendar on a parent's account dedicated to that child's events, or use Home Assistant's **Local Calendar** integration to create a calendar entity for them.
- **Multiple calendars per person**: Only one calendar entity can be assigned per member. If a person uses multiple calendars, consider merging them into one or picking the primary one.

## Editing a family member

1. Open the Family Members modal (gear icon in sidebar)
2. Find the member you want to edit
3. Click the **pencil icon** on their card
4. The form opens with the member's current data pre-filled
5. Modify any fields
6. Click **Save Changes**

---

## Removing a family member

1. Open the Family Members modal
2. Find the member you want to remove
3. Click the **trash icon** on their card
4. The button changes to show **Confirm?** (you have 3 seconds to click again)
5. Click **Confirm?** to permanently remove the member

Removing a member does not delete their chore completions or streak history. However, they will no longer appear in the Chores panel or Leaderboard.

## Data storage

Family member data is stored in the browser's localStorage under the key `beacon_family_members`. When a Home Assistant client is connected, Beacon attempts to sync the data to an `input_text.beacon_family_members` helper entity (for payloads under 255 characters).

For larger families, the data may exceed the HA input_text limit. In that case, localStorage is the sole storage location. This means:

- Family members are tied to the specific browser/device where they were created
- Clearing browser data will remove family members
- To use the same family on multiple displays, set up members on each device separately

> **Tip**: For a production setup, consider using a dedicated tablet that is always on, with browser data persistence enabled.

## Troubleshooting

### Family members disappear after clearing browser data

Family data is stored in localStorage. If you clear browser data (cache, cookies, site data), family members will be lost. To avoid this:
- Use a kiosk browser (like Fully Kiosk) that does not clear data
- Avoid clearing site data for your Beacon URL

### The "Add Chore" button does not appear

The Add Chore button appears when there is at least one family member defined. If no members exist, the Chores panel shows "Add family members first to assign chores."

### Changes do not sync across devices

Each device has its own localStorage. Family members added on one tablet will not appear on another. This is a known limitation of the localStorage-based storage approach.
