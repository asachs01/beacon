# A better family command center

Beacon is an open-source, self-hosted family dashboard built for wall-mounted displays. It gives your household a single, glanceable view of the day — calendars, chores, weather, music, photos, and more — without a subscription or a dedicated vendor display.

Beacon runs as a **Home Assistant add-on** or as a standalone **Docker container**, so if you already have a smart home hub, you are most of the way there.

---

## Feature overview

### Weekly calendar with drag-and-drop
View the entire week at a glance. Create, edit, and delete events directly on the display. Drag events to reschedule them. Recurring events (daily, weekly, monthly) are supported out of the box. Each calendar gets its own color so you can tell family members apart from across the room.

### Dashboard with clock, weather, and tasks
The dashboard view shows a large clock, a personalized greeting ("Good morning, Sachs family"), today's events, current weather with a 5-day forecast, a task checklist, and a countdown widget for upcoming milestones.

### Family members and chores
Add up to 8 family members with custom avatars (55+ emoji options across 5 categories), colors, roles (parent or child), and optional PINs. Assign chores with dollar values and track completion streaks on a leaderboard.

### Grocery lists
Connect Grocy or AnyList through Home Assistant to manage shopping lists directly from the display. Quick-add items, check them off, and get alerts when products are about to expire. Today's meals appear on the dashboard Menu card, which is editable even on empty days, and you can plan breakfast, lunch, dinner, and snacks on the week grid in the [Meals](/docs/features/meals/) ([v1.48.0](https://github.com/asachs01/beacon/releases/tag/v1.48.0)) view (the grid follows Settings → General → Week Starts On). You can also fill the plan from the AnyList sync or the MCP meal tools. From a recipe, Add ingredients to grocery list writes to the default list or the built-in Shopping List, including Home Assistant to-do lists; it does not write to Grocy. Grocy's own meal plan also does not feed the Menu card.

### Music controls
Control any Music Assistant or media player entity in Home Assistant. See album art, track info, and a progress bar. Switch between multiple speakers. A persistent "Now Playing" bar appears at the bottom of any view when music is playing.

### Photo frame
Turn your display into a digital photo frame. Load photos from your Home Assistant media directory. The slideshow auto-cycles with crossfade transitions, shows a clock and date overlay, and can display music controls on top of photos.

### 7 built-in themes
Choose from Skylight (default light), Midnight (dark), Nord Ice, Dracula, Monokai, Rose, and Forest. An "Auto" mode switches between light and dark based on time of day (6 AM / 7 PM). Themes are applied via CSS custom properties, so creating your own is straightforward.

### Timer and stopwatch
A slide-out panel with countdown timer (1, 5, 10, 15, 30 minute presets) and stopwatch mode with lap tracking. Audio alerts sound when the countdown finishes.

### Notifications
Browser push notifications and Home Assistant mobile app notifications fire 10 minutes before calendar events by default (you can change this to 5, 10, 15, or 30 minutes in **Settings > Calendar**). No configuration needed beyond granting browser permission.

### Screen saver and burn-in protection
After 5 minutes of inactivity the screen dims. After 10 minutes a clock screensaver appears with the time and date, repositioning every 30 seconds to prevent burn-in. Any touch or movement wakes the display.

### Self-hosted and private
Beacon is self-hosted: it runs on your Home Assistant or your own server, with no Beacon account, no Beacon cloud, and no telemetry. Calendar data flows from Google/CalDAV through Home Assistant to Beacon, and Beacon doesn't send it anywhere else. Features you connect to outside services, such as a cloud Assist agent, an AI Task for photo import, or Google Photos shared albums, send data to those services. Keep Beacon on your trusted home network; see [Is it safe to expose Beacon to the internet?](/docs/reference/faq/#is-it-safe-to-expose-beacon-to-the-internet).

### Free forever
Beacon is open source under the MIT license. No subscriptions, no premium tiers, no "free trial" that expires.

## How it compares

Beacon is a free, MIT-licensed family command center you install yourself. Skylight Calendar and Hearth Display are dedicated wall displays with paid plans for many family features, and DAKboard is a hosted screen service with a free plan and paid tiers.

Competitor prices and features change, so the sourced tables live on [Beacon vs Skylight](/docs/getting-started/skylight-alternative/) and [Beacon vs Skylight, Hearth, and DAKboard](/docs/getting-started/compare/). Those pages only include claims checked against each company's own site.
